Help

  1. Select an installed font, open a font file, or choose Draw.
  2. Apply a style preset and use the Pixel editor tab to adjust individual glyphs and advances.
  3. Choose Copy Sprite Font, paste it in your Construct 3 layout and click to place it.

Accessibility

In the preview tabs, use the arrow keys to switch between the atlas and the pixel editor.
